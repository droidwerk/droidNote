## What needs a new name

If you distribute a modified version of DroidNote, or offer it to other people in any form, give it its own name and logo, and remove the DroidNote and DroidWerk marks from the app, installer, website and store listings. A note such as "based on DroidNote by DroidWerk" is welcome. Do not use the marks in a way that suggests DroidWerk made, endorsed or supports your version.
